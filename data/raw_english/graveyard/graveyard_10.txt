The memorial was held at the grave site as the deceased had requested. Forty people gathered on a grey November morning.

During the service three people independently reported the same thing afterward. They had seen someone standing at the back of the gathered mourners. A person they did not recognize. Tall. Still. Looking not at the coffin but at the family.

When asked to describe the person they each gave the same description. Down to the clothes. Down to the expression. Calm. Patient. Like someone waiting for the right moment to speak.

The widow was told about this afterward. She listened carefully. She asked for the description again.

Then she was quiet for a long moment.

She said that was my husband's father. He died before I ever met him. But I have seen photographs.

She said he always told me his father never missed anything important in his family's life. She said apparently that still holds.
