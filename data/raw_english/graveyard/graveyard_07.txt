The children in the neighborhood had invented a game. Dare each other to touch the black gravestone in the old cemetery. The one without a name. Just a date. Eighteen ninety one.

Nobody knew whose it was. Nobody in the neighborhood had ever known.

One October night four children went together. Strength in numbers. They touched the stone one by one. Nothing happened. They laughed about how stupid they had been to be afraid.

On the walk home they started disappearing.

Not vanishing. Just falling behind. One by one. Each one stopping to look at something in the darkness. Each one failing to catch up.

One child made it home. The youngest. She said the others had each stopped and looked at the stone even though the stone was behind them.

She said they looked at it and smiled and went back.

The three children were found in the cemetery at dawn. Sitting around the black stone. Awake. Fine. Unharmed.

None of them would say what had kept them there all night.
