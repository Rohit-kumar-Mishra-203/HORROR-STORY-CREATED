The cemetery restoration project brought volunteers every weekend. Kate was one of them. She cleaned old stones. Recorded names and dates. Tried to give the forgotten dead some dignity.

She had been doing it for six months when she found the section nobody had mapped. Far back against the old stone wall. Stones so weathered the names were gone. Some fallen. Some just gone.

She started cleaning them anyway. No names to record. Just the act of cleaning.

On her fourth weekend there she arrived to find her work from the previous week undone. Stones she had stood upright were tipped again. Moss she had removed was back.

She cleaned them again. The following week the same.

On her fifth return she arrived early. Before anyone else. She sat and waited.

At eight in the morning she heard something. Coming from the direction of the unmapped section. She walked toward it.

Every stone she had cleaned was lying flat. And carved into the ground in front of each one were fresh marks. She bent to read them. Three words. Repeated at each stone. Leave us alone.
