The last thing he wrote in his journal before he died was bury me facing east. His family did not know why but they honored the request.

Six months after his burial strange things began near his grave. Other graves nearby showed signs of disturbance. The groundskeeper reported that the grass around his stone was always dead by morning no matter how it looked the night before.

A year after his burial a local historian looked into his past. What she found she did not publish. She went to the cemetery instead. She stood at his grave for a long time.

She said quietly I know what you did.

The air around the grave went cold. She felt it. Like a held breath.

She said you are being kept here until you answer for it. She said I cannot help you with that. But I can tell you that it is not up to me.

She walked away.

Behind her she heard the sound of earth moving. She did not turn around. She walked to her car and drove home and did not come back.

The groundskeeper noted the next morning that the grave had shifted. The stone was now facing west.
