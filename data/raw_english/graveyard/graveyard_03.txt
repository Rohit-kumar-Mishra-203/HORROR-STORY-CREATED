Helen visited her husband's grave every Sunday without fail. Twenty years she had been doing this. She knew every stone in that cemetery by name.

One Sunday she noticed a new grave. Fresh. Unmarked. No stone yet. Just turned earth and a small wooden cross with no name.

She asked the cemetery office about it. They had no record of a new burial that week.

She went back the following Sunday. The grave was still there. Still unmarked. But the earth had been disturbed. Something had been digging at it from above.

The third Sunday she brought her neighbor who had been a police officer. He looked at the grave carefully. He said the disturbing is from below. Not above.

They reported it to the authorities. When investigators opened the grave it was empty. Always had been. The coffin was there. The coffin was sealed. But inside was nothing.

Nobody ever found out who had dug the grave or why. Or what had been pressing against the lid from inside.
