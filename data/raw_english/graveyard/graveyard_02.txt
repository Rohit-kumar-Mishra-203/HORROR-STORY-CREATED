The groundskeeper had worked the cemetery for thirty years. He had a rule he never broke. Never walk the eastern section after three in the morning.

A new assistant asked him why.

He thought about it for a long time before answering. He said the eastern section is the oldest part. Some of those graves go back two hundred years. And some of what is in them has had a very long time to figure out how to get out.

The assistant laughed. The groundskeeper did not.

Three months later the assistant stayed late to finish some work. It got dark earlier than he expected. He cut through the eastern section on his way to the gate.

The groundskeeper found him at the eastern fence in the morning. He was alive. He was sitting with his back against the fence and his knees pulled to his chest. He would not say what he had seen. He would not say anything at all for three days.

When he finally spoke his first words were I understand about the eastern section now.
