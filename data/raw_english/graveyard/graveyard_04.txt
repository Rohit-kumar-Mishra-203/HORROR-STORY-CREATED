The photograph was old. Black and white. A family standing in front of a gravestone. Father. Mother. Three children. All serious in the way people were serious in photographs from that era.

Marcus found it in a box at an estate sale. He bought the box without looking through it carefully. When he got home and found the photograph he almost threw it away.

Instead he set it on his desk.

That night he dreamed of the family. Standing in a cemetery. Looking at him.

In the morning the photograph had changed. The family was still there. But they were no longer looking at the camera. They were looking at something to the left of the frame.

Three days later they were looking at something below them.

A week after that the photograph showed only the gravestone. The family was gone. On the gravestone was a name he recognized as his own.
