The messages started on a Thursday. Unknown number. A single line of text.

I can see your light on.

She looked out her window. The street was empty.

She did not respond.

Friday. Different unknown number. Same message.

I can see your light on.

Saturday. Three different numbers. Same message. All sent at the same time.

She called the police. They said there was nothing they could do. No threat had been made.

Sunday. Twenty seven numbers. Same message. Sent simultaneously.

She turned off all her lights.

A minute later her phone lit up. All twenty seven numbers again.

The message said we can still see you.
