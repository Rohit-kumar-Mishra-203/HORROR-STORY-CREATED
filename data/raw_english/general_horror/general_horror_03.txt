The cabin had been in the family for thirty years. She had visited every summer as a child. She knew every board and corner.

She went alone for the first time. A week by herself. To think.

The first two days were peaceful. The third night she heard footsteps on the porch.

She looked out. Nothing.

The fourth night the footsteps were inside. Moving through the cabin while she lay still in her bed.

She said who is there.

The footsteps stopped.

She got up and turned on every light. Nothing.

She called her mother. She described what was happening. Her mother was quiet for a moment.

Then her mother said I should have told you something before you went.

She asked what.

Her mother said the cabin is fine during the day. Your grandfather said there was something there that only moved at night. He said it had always been there. Long before the cabin. He said it was not dangerous but that it did not like to be seen.

She asked why nobody had told her.

Her mother said because we did not think you would go alone.

She said so what do I do.

Her mother said what your grandfather did. Turn the lights off and pretend to be asleep. It will leave you alone.

She tried it. The footsteps continued for an hour. Then stopped.

She did not sleep. But nothing harmed her.

In the morning she packed her things and drove home. She has not been back.
