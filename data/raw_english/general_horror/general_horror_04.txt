The painting was left behind by the previous tenant. The landlord said to dispose of it however she liked.

She liked it. A dark landscape. Moonlit. A figure in the distance too small to make out clearly.

She hung it in her living room.

A month later she noticed the figure was closer than it had been. She was certain. The proportions of the landscape had not changed. But the figure was now large enough to make out roughly. A person. Standing still. Facing the viewer.

She mentioned it to a friend who said she was imagining it.

Another month. The figure was at the edge of the moonlit area now. Almost at the foreground. Close enough that she could see it was looking at her.

She took the painting down. Stored it in her closet.

She could hear it in there. Not loudly. A faint sound. Like breathing. Like patience.

She drove to the edge of the city and threw it in a dumpster.

She went home and felt immediately that something was different about her apartment. She went to her bedroom.

The wall where the painting had hung.

The figure was there. On the wall. No paint. No frame. Just the figure. Standing in the plaster. Facing her.
