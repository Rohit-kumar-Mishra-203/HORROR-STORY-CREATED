The phone rang at three in the morning. He answered it without thinking. A reflex.

Silence on the other end. Then breathing. Slow and steady. Not threatening. Just there.

He said hello.

The breathing continued.

He said who is this.

The breathing stopped. There was a pause. Then a voice. His own voice. Saying his own name back to him.

He ended the call.

He sat in the dark for a long time. He told himself it was a recording. Someone with his voice. A prank.

The phone rang again.

He let it ring.

It rang fourteen times and stopped.

In the morning he checked his call history. The incoming number was his own number. His own phone. Calling itself.

He called his carrier. They said this was not possible.

He changed his number.

Three nights later the new number received a call at three in the morning. His own new number calling itself.

He answered it.

His own voice said you should not have changed it.
