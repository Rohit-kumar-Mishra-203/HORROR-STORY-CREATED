The recording was found on an old tape player in an estate sale. Nobody knew who it had belonged to.

The buyer played it at home. A voice. Male. Reading what sounded like a list.

Names. Addresses. Dates.

He listened to the whole tape. An hour long. Names and addresses and dates. All historical. All from decades ago.

He looked up some of the names. The dates on the tape matched dates of disappearances from old newspaper archives he found online.

He kept looking. Every name on the tape matched a real disappearance. A real person. A real date.

There were two hundred and fourteen names on the tape.

At the end of the list the voice paused. Then it said that concludes the current roster.

Another pause.

Then it said next entry.

Then the tape ended.

He counted the names again. Two hundred and fourteen.

He looked up the tape player. The model had been manufactured beginning in a specific year.

The earliest date on the tape was fourteen years before the tape player had been manufactured.
