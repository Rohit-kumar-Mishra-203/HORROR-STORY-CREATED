She had always been sensitive to atmosphere. She walked into rooms and felt things others did not. She had learned to be quiet about it.

The new office building was wrong from the first day. Not dramatically. Not obviously. Just a persistent low level sense that something in the building was watching the people in it.

She kept this to herself.

Three months in she mentioned it to a colleague she trusted. He laughed. She expected that.

Six months in the building's turnover rate was three times higher than comparable offices. People leaving without clear reasons. Some citing stress. Some citing unease they could not specify.

A year in she was one of four original employees remaining.

She stayed because she wanted to understand it.

One evening working late she felt it change. The watching feeling had always been distant. Observational. Like being studied by something curious and patient.

That evening it was different. Closer. More focused.

She said out loud I know you are there. She said I have always known.

The feeling intensified. She had the impression of attention. Pure and total.

She said what do you want.

The lights went out.

In the dark she heard something that might have been language. Very old. Very slow. Like something that had been waiting for someone to ask that question for a very long time and was now trying to remember how to answer.
