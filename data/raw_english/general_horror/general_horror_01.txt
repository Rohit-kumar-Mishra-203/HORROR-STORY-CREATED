Something was wrong with the neighborhood. Cole had felt it since he moved in but could not name what it was.

The neighbors were friendly. The houses were well kept. The street was quiet. Too quiet maybe. No dogs barking at night. No cats crossing the road. No birds in the trees.

He asked his neighbor about it one morning. She smiled and said it has always been like this. This is a peaceful street.

The smile did not reach her eyes.

That night Cole looked out his bedroom window at two in the morning. All his neighbors were outside. Standing in their yards facing the same direction. Down the street toward the empty lot at the end of the block.

They were completely still. Not speaking. Not moving. Just standing and looking at the darkness.

Cole watched them for twenty minutes. Not one of them moved.

In the morning he saw his neighbor again. She asked how he was sleeping. He said fine.

She said good. She said you will sleep better soon. They all do eventually.

Cole listed his house for sale that afternoon.
