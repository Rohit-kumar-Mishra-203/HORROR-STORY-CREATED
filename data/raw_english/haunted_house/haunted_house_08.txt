The village had one old house that nobody talked about. Children were told to cross to the other side of the road when passing it. Adults did not need to be told.

Priya was a journalist. She rented the house specifically because nobody talked about it.

The first night nothing happened. She was almost disappointed.

The second night she heard music. Old music. From the walls. She recorded it on her phone. When she played it back the music was not there. Just silence and the sound of her own breathing.

The third night she found writing on the bathroom mirror. Condensation letters. She read them before they faded. They said go home.

She wrote in her notebook. Something knows I'm here.

The fourth night she packed her bag to leave. In the morning. Just until the article was done.

She woke at three to find someone had unpacked her bag. Every item returned to exactly where it had been. As if she had never packed at all.
