The renovation crew found the room behind the wall on a Wednesday morning. Sealed from the inside. No door. No window. Just a room that had been closed off and forgotten sometime in the nineteen thirties.

Inside was furniture. A bed. A chair. A small writing desk. Everything covered in decades of dust.

And on the desk a journal. The last entry dated November fourteenth nineteen thirty one. The handwriting deteriorated toward the end. The last readable line said I can hear them in the walls and I no longer know if I put myself in here or if something put me here.

The crew chief took the journal home. Three nights later he called his supervisor at two in the morning and said he could hear something in his walls.

The journal was found on his kitchen table when his family checked on him in the morning. He was not there. The last page had a new entry. Same handwriting as the original. But the ink was fresh.
