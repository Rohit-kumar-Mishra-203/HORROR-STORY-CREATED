The farmhouse had been in the family for four generations. Marcus inherited it when his grandmother died. He had not visited since childhood.

He found the house exactly as he remembered it. Except for one thing. There was a door at the end of the upstairs hallway that he had no memory of.

He asked his mother over the phone. She said there had always been a door there. He must have forgotten.

He opened the door. A room. Empty. A single window facing the back field.

He went in. The door closed behind him. When he turned the handle it would not open.

He spent four hours in that room. Shouting. Banging on the door. His phone had no signal.

At exactly midnight the door swung open on its own.

He walked out into the hallway. The house felt different. He went downstairs and called his mother.

She answered immediately. She said Marcus where have you been. It has been three days. He said no. It has been four hours.

There was a long silence. Then she said your grandmother used to say that room takes time. She never explained what she meant.
