The hotel had a reputation. Room two fourteen. Every guest who stayed in it reported the same thing. Waking at three in the morning. A figure standing in the corner. Gone when the light was turned on.

Management had tried everything. New furniture. New paint. A priest had been brought in twice.

Nothing changed. The figure kept appearing.

A paranormal researcher booked the room for a week. He set up cameras. Motion sensors. Temperature monitors. He was going to prove or disprove once and for all.

On the first night at three in the morning his equipment registered a temperature drop of twelve degrees in the corner. The camera showed nothing. But the motion sensor triggered.

On the second night the same. On the third night his camera caught a brief interference. Three seconds of static at three oh three. When he analyzed the static he found a pattern. Repeated. Consistent.

It took him two days to realize the pattern was morse code. When he decoded it the message said simply I know you are watching me too.
