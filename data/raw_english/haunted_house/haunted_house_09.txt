The house had good bones said the inspector. He did not elaborate. He signed the forms and left quickly.

In the first month Anna noticed the cold spots. Certain places in the house that were always cold regardless of the heating. The corner of the living room. The top of the stairs. The spot beside the kitchen sink.

She got used to them. Started to think of them as just features of the house.

Then her daughter started standing in the cold spots. Just standing there. Not playing. Not doing anything. Just standing and looking at nothing.

Anna asked her what she was doing.

She said I'm standing with the people.

Anna said what people.

She said the ones who live here too. She said they were here first. She said they are not mean. They are just sad.

Anna knelt down and said what are they sad about.

Her daughter thought for a moment. She said they can't remember how to leave.
