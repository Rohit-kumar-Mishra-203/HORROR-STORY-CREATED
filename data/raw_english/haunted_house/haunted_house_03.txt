Nobody had lived in the manor for sixty years. The caretaker came once a week. He never stayed after four in the afternoon. He had never explained why and nobody had ever asked.

When the new owners took possession they found the caretaker's logbook going back forty years. Every entry was the same format. Date. Time of arrival. Time of departure. Brief notes on the condition of the property.

Every single entry had the same departure time. Three fifty eight. Without exception. For forty years.

They asked the caretaker about it. He was an old man by then. He looked at them for a long moment before answering.

He said there are things in that house that sleep during the day. They begin to wake around four. I have been doing this for forty years and I have never wanted to be there when they are fully awake.

The new owners thanked him. They moved in on a Saturday. By the following Thursday only one of them remained. The other had gone somewhere that she could not explain and that she did not seem to want to leave.
