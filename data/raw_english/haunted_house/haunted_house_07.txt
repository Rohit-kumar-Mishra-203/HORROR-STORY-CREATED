The estate agent called it a project property. What she meant was that something was wrong with it and she could not legally specify what.

Tom was a contractor. He had renovated dozens of old houses. He was not afraid of rotten beams or outdated wiring. He bought the house.

The renovation went normally for the first two weeks. Then his crew started leaving early. Different reasons every day. Never the real reason.

By the third week he was working alone.

On a Thursday afternoon he was pulling up floorboards in the master bedroom when he found the photographs. Dozens of them. Hidden under the floor. All showing the same thing. The interior of the house. Every room. Every angle.

The photographs were dated. They spanned forty years. Different furniture in different eras. Different wallpaper.

In every single photograph a figure stood in the background. The same figure. Unchanged across forty years. Watching whoever had taken the picture.

Tom looked up from the photographs. His work light cast shadows across the empty room.

One of the shadows did not belong to him.
