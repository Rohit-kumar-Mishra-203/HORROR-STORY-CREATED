Jake bought the house sight unseen because the price was too good to pass up. When he arrived he understood the price.

It was not the condition of the house. It was well maintained. It was the feeling. An immediate conviction as he stepped through the front door that he was being watched. Not by a person. By the house itself.

He told himself this was nonsense and moved his boxes in.

The first night he heard the floors creak in a pattern that sounded like footsteps. Starting at the far end of the hallway and moving toward his bedroom door. Stopping there.

The second night he put flour on the hallway floor before bed. In the morning the flour showed footprints. Coming from the far end of the hallway. Stopping at his door. And then returning.

Whatever had come to his door had thought about it. And gone back.
