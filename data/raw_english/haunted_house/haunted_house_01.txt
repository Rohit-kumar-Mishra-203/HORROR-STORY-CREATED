The house had been on the market for eleven years. Every buyer who walked through it left quickly and never explained why. The real estate agent had stopped asking.

Sarah was different. She was a skeptic. She bought the house because it was half the price of anything else in the neighborhood.

The first week was fine. The second week the doors started opening on their own. She told herself it was the foundation settling. Old houses did that.

The third week she found the basement door open when she was certain she had never opened it. She went down. The basement was empty except for one thing. A chair in the center of the room facing the stairs. As if someone had been sitting there. Waiting. Watching the door.

Sarah stood at the bottom of the stairs and felt a certainty settle over her that was worse than fear. She was not alone in this house. She had never been alone in this house. And whatever shared it with her had been waiting a very long time for someone to finally come downstairs.
