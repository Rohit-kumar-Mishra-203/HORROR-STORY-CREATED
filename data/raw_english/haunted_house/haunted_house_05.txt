The bed and breakfast had wonderful reviews. Charming. Historic. Unforgettable. Clara booked the blue room for a long weekend.

The room was indeed charming. Old furniture. High ceilings. A window that looked out on the garden.

She woke the first night to find a woman sitting in the chair by the window. Old. Very still. Looking out at the garden.

Clara sat up and said excuse me.

The woman did not respond.

Clara turned on the bedside lamp. The chair was empty.

In the morning she mentioned it to the owner at breakfast. He smiled politely and said the previous owner of the house had loved that garden. She had sat in that chair every evening for fifty years.

Clara said and when did she die.

The owner said in that chair. Looking at the garden. About fifteen years ago.

He refilled her coffee. He asked if she had slept well. She said yes. She did not mention that when she had turned off the lamp the night before the woman had been back in the chair. Still looking at the garden. But this time her head had been turned slightly. Toward Clara.
