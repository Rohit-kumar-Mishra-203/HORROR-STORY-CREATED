The painting came from an estate auction. A landscape. Nothing unusual about the subject. Rolling hills. A farmhouse in the distance. Grey sky.

What was unusual was the price. Far below what a painting of that size and age should have cost. The auctioneer had moved past it quickly.

He hung it in his study. He liked it.

After two weeks he noticed the farmhouse in the painting was closer than it had been. Not dramatically closer. Just slightly. As if the perspective had shifted.

He checked his memory. He was certain it had been further.

A month later the farmhouse was much closer. Now occupying the center of the painting. The hills had receded.

He did not remember this being the composition.

After three months the farmhouse filled almost the entire canvas. The door was open. Inside the open door was darkness.

He took the painting down and stored it facing the wall.

Two nights later he woke to the sound of a door opening. In the room with the painting. He went in. The painting was facing outward.

In the darkness of the painted doorway something was looking out.
