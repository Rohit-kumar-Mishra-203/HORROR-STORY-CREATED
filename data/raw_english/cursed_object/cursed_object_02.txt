The ring had been in the family for four generations. Each generation had been told the same thing. Do not wear it. Keep it in the box. Do not let anyone take it out.

Mara had heard the warning her whole life. When her grandmother died and she inherited the box she thought the warning was superstition.

She took the ring out. She put it on.

It felt warm. Not the warmth of metal that has been held. A different warmth. Alive.

She wore it for three days before she noticed she was dreaming other people's memories. Not her own. The memories of strangers from different eras. She woke from them disoriented. Unsure for a moment which life was hers.

On the fourth day she tried to take the ring off. It came off easily. She put it back in the box.

That night she dreamed of a woman she did not know who looked directly at her in the dream and said thank you for the time. She said I will need more soon.

Mara sealed the box with tape. She put the box in a drawer. She put the drawer key somewhere she would not easily find it.

Two months later she found the box open on her bedside table. The ring was back on her finger. She had no memory of putting it there.
