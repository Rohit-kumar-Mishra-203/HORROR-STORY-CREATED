The mirror had been in the antique shop for years. The owner said it had come in as part of an estate lot and he had never been able to sell it. He did not say why.

She bought it because it was beautiful. Ornate frame. Old glass with that slight distortion that old mirrors have.

The first strange thing was small. She noticed her reflection blinked a half second after she blinked. She thought it was a trick of the light.

The second strange thing was harder to dismiss. She raised her right hand. Her reflection raised its left. Which is normal for a mirror. But it raised it slightly before she did.

After that she started watching carefully.

The reflection was always a beat ahead of her. As if it knew what she was going to do before she did it.

A month after she bought the mirror she woke in the night to find herself standing in front of it. She had no memory of getting up. She was wearing different clothes than she had gone to bed in.

Her reflection was wearing the clothes she had gone to bed in. And it looked back at her with an expression of something between pity and apology.
