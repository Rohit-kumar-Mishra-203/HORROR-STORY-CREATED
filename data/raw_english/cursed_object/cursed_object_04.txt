The doll had belonged to his grandmother. He found it in a box when clearing out her house. Porcelain. Old. Eyes that were too realistic for comfort.

He almost threw it away. He kept it instead because she had kept it.

The first night it was in his house he woke at three in the morning to the sound of something moving in the spare room where he had put it.

He opened the door. The doll was in a different position than he had left it.

He told himself he had placed it differently than he remembered.

The following night the same sound. The doll in a different position.

He set up a camera in the spare room.

In the morning he reviewed the footage. At two forty seven the doll's head turned. Slowly. Toward the camera. It stopped when it was facing the lens directly.

Then it was still for the rest of the night.

He called his cousin who had also helped clear the grandmother's house. He asked if she knew anything about the doll.

His cousin said actually yes. She said grandmother always kept it in a locked cabinet. She never explained why. She said she found it once as a child outside the cabinet and grandmother was more frightened than she had ever seen her.
