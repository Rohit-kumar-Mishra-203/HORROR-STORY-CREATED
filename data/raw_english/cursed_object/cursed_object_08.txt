The watch had stopped at eleven forty three. That was how he bought it. A stopped watch at a flea market.

He got it running again. A watchmaker did it in twenty minutes. Said it was a simple fix.

The watch ran fine. Good time. He wore it every day.

A month later he noticed it was running fast. Gaining about a minute a day. He took it back to the watchmaker.

The watchmaker examined it. He said I cannot explain this. He said the mechanism is correct. There is no reason for it to gain time.

The watch kept gaining. A minute a day became five minutes a day. Five became ten.

After three months the watch was running six hours fast.

He stopped wearing it. Kept it in a box.

He found himself anxious when it was out of sight. A low continuous unease. Like forgetting something important.

He started wearing it again.

He has had it for three years now. It runs approximately three days fast. He has adjusted his life around it. He shows up three days early to everything.

He has started to believe the watch is not running fast. He has started to believe it is running correctly. And that everyone else's time is what is wrong.
