The music box was beautiful. Brass and ivory with a dancer on top that spun when the lid was opened. Clara found it at an estate sale and paid almost nothing for it.

The music it played was not any melody she recognized. Slow and minor and somehow wrong in a way she could not identify.

She noticed the first change after three days. The dancer was facing a different direction than it had been. She was certain of this. She had looked at it carefully when she bought it.

After a week she found the music box open on her kitchen table in the morning. She lived alone and she had not opened it.

After two weeks the melody began playing on its own at three in the morning. She would wake to the sound of it drifting through the dark apartment.

She tried to throw it away. She put it in a garbage bag and carried it to the dumpster down the street. In the morning it was on her kitchen table again.

The lid was open. The dancer was spinning. And the melody it was playing had changed. It sounded like her name.
