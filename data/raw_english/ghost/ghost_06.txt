The house sitter job was supposed to be easy. Two weeks. Feed the cat. Water the plants. Do not go into the basement.

Sarah asked why not the basement. The owner said the door sticks and it is not worth the trouble. Sarah accepted this.

The first week was fine. The cat did not like to be in certain rooms. It would stop at doorways and refuse to enter. Sarah thought this was quirky and charming.

The second week the cat disappeared. Sarah looked everywhere. She checked every room. Every closet. The cat was gone.

On the eleventh night she heard it from the basement. A sound like crying. High and thin. Like a cat but also not quite like a cat.

She opened the basement door. The stairs went down into darkness. The sound stopped immediately.

She went down anyway. The basement was empty. Clean concrete floor. One bare bulb. Nothing else.

She turned to go back up. The door at the top of the stairs was closed. And on the other side something was scratching to get in.
