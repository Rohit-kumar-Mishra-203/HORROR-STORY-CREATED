The school photograph had been taken in nineteen sixty two. Thirty children in three rows. A teacher at each end.

Lena found it in a box of things from her grandmother's attic. She set it on her desk.

That night she woke to the sound of children singing. Faint. Coming from downstairs. A song she did not know.

In the morning she checked the photograph. All thirty children were in the same positions. But the teacher on the right end was gone. The space where she had stood was just blank now. Just wall behind an empty gap in the row.

The next night the singing again. Louder.

In the morning another child was missing from the photograph.

Lena counted the remaining children every morning. She does not tell anyone what she is watching for. She just counts. And every few days there is one less.
