Maya had been documenting abandoned places for three years. She had a rule. Never stay after dark. She broke the rule once.

The old hospital had been closed for forty years. She went in at noon. Lost track of time. By the time she noticed it was dark outside the front door had jammed.

She had her camera. She had her phone. She started documenting to stay calm.

The photographs showed things the rooms did not seem to contain. A figure in the corridor. A face at the window of a room on the third floor when she was on the ground floor. A hand on her shoulder in a room she was certain was empty.

Her phone died at midnight.

She sat in the dark for six hours. Something moved through the building. She heard it on every floor. Sometimes close. Sometimes far. Always moving.

At six in the morning the front door opened by itself. Maya walked out into the grey morning light and did not look back.

She never developed the photographs. She deleted them all. Some things are not meant to be documented.
