The grief counselor said it was normal to feel a presence after losing someone. Ryan nodded and went home and sat in the dark.

His brother had been dead for two weeks. He had been there when it happened. He had watched the life leave his brother's face. He knew what dead looked like.

Which was why it was so disturbing when his brother called him at two in the morning.

He did not answer the first time. He told himself it was someone using his brother's old phone. A mistake. Crossed lines.

The second call came an hour later. He answered.

His brother's voice. Exactly right. The particular way he said Ryan's name that nobody else had ever replicated.

He said I don't know where I am. It's dark here. I can't find anything I recognize.

Ryan said I don't know how to help you.

His brother said I know. I just didn't want to be alone.

The call ended. Ryan sat until morning. The phone never rang again.
