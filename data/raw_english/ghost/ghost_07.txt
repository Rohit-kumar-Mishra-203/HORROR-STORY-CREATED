The photograph on the mantle showed a family of four. Mother. Father. Two children. All smiling in a garden.

Marcus bought the house without knowing anything about the previous owners. The photograph was left behind. He meant to throw it away.

He got used to it. Stopped noticing it.

Until one morning he walked past and the photograph had changed. One of the children was no longer smiling. She was looking directly at the camera with an expression that did not belong on a child's face.

He checked the photograph carefully. It was the same photograph. Old paper. Original frame. But the expression had changed.

The next morning the girl in the photograph had turned. She was no longer facing the camera. She was facing the other child.

Three days later both children were turned away from the camera. Facing the parents.

Marcus removed the photograph. Put it face down in a drawer.

That night he heard crying from the drawer. He did not open it. In the morning the photograph was back on the mantle. All four of them staring directly at him.
