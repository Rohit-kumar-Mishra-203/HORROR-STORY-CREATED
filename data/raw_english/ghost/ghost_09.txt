The child started talking to someone who was not there. This worried her parents but the doctors said imaginary friends were normal.

The child called her friend Emily. She said Emily lived in the wall of her bedroom. She said Emily had been there a long time and was very lonely.

Her parents were patient. They said goodnight to Emily. They set a place at the table for Emily. They treated it as charming.

Then one night her father heard two voices from his daughter's room. He stood outside the door and listened. His daughter's voice and another voice. Smaller. Older. Wrong in a way he could not place.

He opened the door. His daughter was sitting up in bed talking to the corner of the room.

He said who are you talking to.

She said Emily. I told you.

He looked at the corner. He could see nothing. But the air there was cold enough to show his breath.

His daughter said don't worry. She says she just wants to stay a little longer. She says it was so lonely before we came.
