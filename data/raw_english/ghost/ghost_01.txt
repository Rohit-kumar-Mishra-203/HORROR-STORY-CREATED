The apartment was cheap because of its history. Daniel knew this when he signed the lease. Three previous tenants had left within a month. The landlord said it was the pipes. Daniel knew it was not the pipes.

The first night he heard footsteps in the hallway. He opened the door. The hallway was empty.

The second night he heard breathing beside his bed. He turned on the light. Nothing.

The third night he woke at exactly three in the morning. There was a woman standing at the foot of his bed. Young. Pale. Her clothes were from another decade. She was looking at him with an expression he could not read.

He said who are you.

She said I used to live here.

He said what do you want.

She said the same thing you want. I want to leave. But I cannot find the door anymore.

Daniel moved out the next day. He never found out what happened to the woman who could not find the door. But sometimes late at night in his new apartment he hears footsteps in the hallway.
