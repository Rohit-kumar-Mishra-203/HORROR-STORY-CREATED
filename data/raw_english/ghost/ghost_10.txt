Tom had lived alone for years. He liked the quiet. He liked knowing that every sound in his apartment came from him.

So when he started hearing a second set of footsteps he noticed immediately.

Not always. Just sometimes. Late at night. The sound of someone walking through the apartment when he was sitting still.

He checked every room every time. Nothing. He had the locks changed. He had the building inspected for wildlife. Nothing.

After three weeks he set up a camera in the living room.

In the morning he watched the footage. The apartment was empty on the screen. But at two forty seven in the morning the footage showed his couch cushions depressing slowly one by one. As if someone was sitting down. Then getting comfortable. Then staying very still for the rest of the night.

Tom watched the footage four times. Then he packed a bag.

At the door he paused. He said out loud I don't know who you are. But this is my home.

On the camera footage which he watched later there was a response. At the moment he spoke every lamp in the apartment flickered once. As if in acknowledgment.
