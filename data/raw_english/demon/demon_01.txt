The book was found in the wall during renovations. Old. Leather bound. Written in a language nobody recognized. Victor kept it because it was interesting.

He spent a week trying to decode it. On the eighth day he found a passage that looked like it might be a phonetic transliteration. He read it aloud without thinking.

The room went cold. The lights flickered once and held. The shadows in the corners moved in a way shadows do not move.

That night Victor dreamed of a dark shape watching him from the ceiling. The next night the shape was closer. The third night he woke to find two red points of light hovering above his bed.

A voice came from everywhere and nowhere. It said you called me. I have been waiting a very long time for someone to call.

Victor said I did not mean to.

The voice laughed. It said that is what they all say. It does not change anything.
