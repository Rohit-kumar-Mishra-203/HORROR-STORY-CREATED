The basement door had been locked when they moved in. The previous owners left no key. A locksmith opened it.

The basement was empty. Clean concrete. One bare bulb. Nothing remarkable.

They used it for storage. Normal things. Boxes. Holiday decorations. Old furniture.

The first sign was the smell. Something that came and went. Not quite identifiable. Not quite anything they had words for.

The second sign was the dog. Their dog would not go near the basement door. Would not look at it. Would leave the room if anyone opened it.

The third sign was what their daughter said one morning at breakfast. She was seven. She said something visits me at night and sits at the end of my bed and watches me.

They asked her to describe it.

She thought about it seriously. She said it looks different every time I look at it. Like it can not decide what to look like.

They sealed the basement door. They had the house blessed. They moved their daughter's room to the other end of the house.

The sitting stopped. But their daughter said sometimes she can still feel it thinking about her from far away.
