The night of the storm the lights failed across the whole neighborhood. Candles only. No phones. No internet. Just darkness and wind and rain.

In the morning when power was restored her neighbor came to check on her. He found her sitting at her kitchen table. Fine. Calm. Unhurt.

But her kitchen table was covered in writing. Every surface. In a language nobody recognized.

She said she had written it during the night. She did not know what it said. She had not been consciously aware of writing it.

A linguist was eventually consulted. He worked on the text for two weeks.

He said it was not a known language. But it followed consistent grammatical rules. It was coherent. It was a message.

He said he could not translate the vocabulary because it had no equivalents.

But he said the structure of the message was clear. It was an introduction. Something was introducing itself. Explaining what it was and where it came from.

The final section he could partially interpret. It said something to the effect of I have been watching this house for a long time and I am glad you finally let me in.
