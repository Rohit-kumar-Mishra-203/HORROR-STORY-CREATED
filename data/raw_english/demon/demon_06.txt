The cult had been using the building for thirty years before anyone found out. When the investigators went through what was left behind they found things that did not fit the standard profile.

Symbols carved into the floors of every room. Not painted. Carved. Deep. As if someone had spent years doing nothing else.

A room in the basement with no door. The wall had been built after the room was already in use. Whatever ceremonies had happened there had happened without any way in or out.

Recordings. Dozens of tapes. The investigators listened to three minutes of the first one before stopping. The sounds on the recordings were not sounds human voices should be able to make.

One investigator stayed longer than the others. She was found in the carved room three hours after the others had left. She was sitting in the center of the room looking at the ceiling.

When they got her out she said she had heard something in the recordings that she recognized. Something she had heard before. A long time ago. In a different context.

She would not say more than that. But she resigned from the investigation the following morning and has not spoken publicly about the case since.
