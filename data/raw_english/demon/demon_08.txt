She found the figure carved into the old oak tree behind the house. She had lived there for five years and never noticed it before.

It was not subtle. Tall. Deliberate. Cut deep into the wood. Something between a human shape and something else.

She photographed it. Showed it to a folklorist friend. Her friend went pale.

She said where did you find this.

She said in my backyard.

Her friend was quiet for a moment. Then she said this is a binding mark. Old. Very old. Someone carved this to keep something contained.

She said contained where.

Her friend said I would rather not speculate. She said when did you first notice changes in your house.

She thought about it. She said about five years ago. When we moved in.

Her friend said and before you moved in.

She said we heard the previous owners left suddenly. They never explained why.

Her friend said I think someone has been re-carving this mark for a very long time. Maintenance. And when you bought the house the maintenance stopped.

She looked at the carving. It was deep. But along some of the lines she could see new wood. Fresh cuts. Recent.

Someone had been tending it right up until they left.
