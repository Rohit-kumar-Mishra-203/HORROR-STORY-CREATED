The deal was simple as these things go. He got what he wanted for twenty years. At the end of twenty years he would pay.

He had been twenty three when he made it. Young enough to feel invincible. Old enough to know what he was doing.

Twenty years passed quickly. They always do when you are getting what you want.

On the last night he sat in his kitchen and waited. He had thought about running. He had thought about finding a way out. He had spent years looking for loopholes.

There were none.

At midnight something came through his walls like smoke. It took a shape. The same shape it had worn twenty years ago when they had first made their arrangement.

It said it is time.

He said I know.

He said can I have one more day.

It said that is not how this works. You know that.

He did. He had always known that. He stood up. He followed it through the wall.

In the morning his family found his kitchen empty. His coffee cold. His chair pushed back as if he had just stood up.

He was never found. But occasionally over the following years the people who had loved him reported dreaming of him. Always the same dream. He was somewhere dark. And he was trying to tell them something they could never quite hear.
