The investigator had seen forty years of strange cases. She had developed a reputation for being unshakeable. Nothing surprised her anymore.

Then she took the case in the old parish house.

She would not write her findings in the official report. She wrote them in a personal letter to her daughter instead. To be opened after her death.

Her daughter read the letter and burned it as instructed. She told no one what it said.

But her daughter's daughter noticed something. In the years after reading the letter her grandmother stopped sleeping in the dark. She kept every light in her house burning all night until she died.

And on the night she finally died in her own bed at ninety one her last words according to the nurse present were tell it I said no.
