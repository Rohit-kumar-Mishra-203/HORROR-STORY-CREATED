The exorcism failed on the first attempt. The priest left shaken. He said whatever was in that house was old. Older than he had encountered before. He said it knew him. Knew his name. Knew things about him that no one should know.

He came back the next day with two other priests. They lasted four hours. When they left the house was quieter but not empty.

The family called a researcher. A woman who had spent thirty years documenting cases like theirs. She came alone.

She stayed for three days. She did not explain what she did in those three days. When she left she said it is gone. Then she said mostly.

The family asked what mostly meant.

She said some things leave but leave a mark. Like a stain. Your house is stained. You can live in a stained house. Most people do without knowing it.

She said the important thing is not to invite it back. She said she would not specify how one might accidentally do that because knowing how is itself an invitation.
