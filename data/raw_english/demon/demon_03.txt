The children in the village began drawing the same thing. Without knowing each other were doing it. All on the same day.

A tall figure. Dark. With hands that reached to the ground. Standing at the edge of a forest.

When the teachers collected the drawings they noticed something else. In every single drawing the figure had a face. But the face was different in each drawing. As if each child had seen it differently.

Or as if it had shown each child a different face.

The village elder was shown the drawings. She looked at them for a long time. Then she said burn them. All of them. Without looking at them again.

When asked why she said because it is listening. And drawings are a form of attention. And this particular thing feeds on attention.

The drawings were burned. The children stopped drawing it.

But that night several adults reported dreaming of a tall dark figure standing at the edge of a forest. With hands that reached to the ground.
