Three nights in a row Ryan woke at the same time. Three fourteen. Not three fifteen. Three fourteen exactly.

The third time he decided to document it. He set up his phone to record.

At three fourteen he woke. He lay still. The room was dark and quiet.

He heard breathing. Not his own. Slower than his own. Coming from the corner of the room.

He did not move. He lay still for eleven minutes until it stopped. Then he slept.

In the morning he reviewed the recording. At three fourteen there was a sound. Not breathing. Something else. Rhythmic. Like breathing but with an undertone that the phone's microphone was not quite equipped to capture.

More importantly at three twenty five the recording showed his bedside lamp turn on by itself. Then off. Then on. Three times.

He had not seen this happen. He had been lying with his eyes closed.

He checked the lamp. It was a simple lamp with a physical switch. The switch had not moved.

He moved the lamp to the other room.

That night he woke at three fourteen. The lamp in the other room was blinking on and off. Three times.
