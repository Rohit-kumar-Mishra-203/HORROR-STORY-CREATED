Story Type: Witch Curse + Haunted Treasure (Chudail ka Shraapit Khazana)
A peaceful, beautiful village…
where evenings echoed with crickets and soft winds…

And in that village lived—
Grandmother…

👵 The Storyteller

Every evening, children gathered around her…

Ravi (excited):
“Grandma! Tell us a witch story!”

Grandmother (uneasy):
“Some stories should not be told…”

But the children insisted…

the wind suddenly grows stronger…

She begins…

🌲 The Ruin

“In a forest… there was a hill…”

leaves crunching…

“A witch lived there… guarding a treasure…”

🪓 The Woodcutter

A woodcutter—Hari—enters the forest…

Hari:
“I’ll find the best wood here…”

He reaches the ruin…

door creaks open slowly…

✨ The Treasure

Inside…

gold coins shining…
diamonds glittering…

Hari:
“This is unbelievable…”

😨 The Trap

BANG!!! door slams shut…
strong winds blow…

Then…

a terrifying laugh…

The witch appears…

👁️ Horror

Witch:
“Greed consumes humans…”

Hari collapses…

🌄 Morning

He wakes up…

not in the ruin…

but outside…

with a gold coin in his pocket…

😱 Truth

He returns home…

“I’m back!”

But…

no one hears him…

He tries to touch water…

his hand passes through…

🩸 Reality

Grandmother whispers—

“He was already dead…”

🌙 End…?

Silence…

Children fall asleep…

👁️ Final Twist

Morning…

Each child holds a gold coin…

Ravi is missing…

Grandmother panics—

“Where is Ravi?!”

A distant echo of laughter from the forest…

“Some stories are not told…
they are invitations…”
