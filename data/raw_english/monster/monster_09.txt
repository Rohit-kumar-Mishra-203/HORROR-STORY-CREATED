The last message from the research station came at three forty in the morning. It was not a distress call. That was what made it strange.

It was a description. Calm and precise. The researcher dictating what she was observing through the station window.

She described something coming across the ice toward the station. Large. White. Moving in a way that was almost right for something living but not entirely.

She described it stopping outside the station. Looking in. She said it appeared to have no face in the way she understood faces to work.

She said it appeared to be communicating. She could not say how she knew this.

She said she was going to open the door.

The recording stopped there.

The rescue team arrived forty eight hours later. The station was intact. Nothing damaged. Nothing taken.

The researcher was gone. There were no tracks outside the station. Not hers. Not anything else.

The ice was perfectly smooth.
