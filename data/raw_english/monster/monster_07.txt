The ranger station had been abandoned for fifteen years. The official reason was budget cuts.

Luke was assigned to reopen it. Routine assignment. Straightforward.

He arrived on a Monday. By Wednesday he understood why the station had been abandoned.

There were tracks around the perimeter. Not any animal he recognized. Too large. Too widely spaced. The stride pattern suggested something bipedal but the foot shape was wrong.

He found marks on the trees. High up. Higher than anything should have been able to reach. Deep gouges. Fresh.

He called his supervisor. He described what he found. His supervisor told him to document everything and submit a report.

He said I think I should leave.

His supervisor said stay until the end of the week.

On Thursday night he heard it outside the station. Moving around the perimeter. Slowly. Not trying to be quiet. As if it wanted him to know it was there.

He submitted his report on Friday morning from the road. He was already driving when he sent it.

The station was not reopened. Budget cuts remained the official explanation.
