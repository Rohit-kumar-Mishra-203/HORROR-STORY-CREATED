The town had a story. Every town has stories but this one was different because it was consistent. The same story told by different families with no connection to each other going back as far as local records existed.

Something lived under the hill at the edge of town. It had always been there. It was dormant most of the time. It woke up periodically. When it woke up people disappeared.

The intervals between wakings were irregular. Sometimes twenty years. Sometimes fifty. Once an interval of nearly a hundred years that the town had allowed itself to believe meant it was gone for good.

It was not gone for good. It woke again. People disappeared again.

A folklorist studied the historical record carefully. She found one consistent detail across all the accounts. The people who disappeared were always people who had recently expressed curiosity about the hill. Who had asked questions. Who had gone to look.

She wrote her analysis. She published it. She included a warning in the final paragraph.

She wrote if you are reading this because you are curious about the hill stop now. The curiosity is the problem. It can hear curiosity. I do not know how. But the record is consistent. The people who disappeared were the ones who wanted to find it. So do not want to find it. Want something else instead.
