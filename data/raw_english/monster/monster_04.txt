The deep sea submersible recorded it at four thousand meters. The footage lasted eleven seconds before the camera was damaged.

The marine biologists who reviewed the footage could not agree on what they were seeing. The scale was wrong. Whatever it was should not have been able to exist at that size. The pressure alone.

The footage was reviewed by three independent teams. All three teams reached the same conclusion. The object was biological. It was alive. It was aware of the submersible.

The footage was classified shortly after the third review.

One of the biologists on the second review team kept a personal copy. She has never released it publicly. She has never described what she saw in detail.

She did say once in an interview that was ostensibly about something else that there are things in the deep ocean that are so far outside our framework of understanding that seeing them does not feel like a scientific discovery. It feels like looking at something that was never meant to be seen. And that has been looking back at you for much longer than you have been looking at it.
