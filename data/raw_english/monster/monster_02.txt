The photographs from the hiking trail camera showed something nobody could explain. The camera had been placed to document wildlife. Deer. Foxes. The occasional bear.

For six months it recorded normal activity.

Then on a Tuesday in November at two seventeen in the morning it recorded something else.

A shape. Moving through the trees at the edge of the frame. Too large for any known animal in the region. Moving in a way that suggested it was trying to stay out of the light.

The shape paused at the edge of the frame. It appeared to look directly at the camera.

Then it reached up and covered the lens with one hand.

The hand was too large for any known primate. The fingers were too long.

The next morning when the researcher went to check the camera she found it on the ground. The mounting post had been pulled out of the earth. Not broken. Pulled. By something strong enough to uproot a six inch steel post.

The camera was intact. The footage was intact.

The researcher filed her report. She requested the trail be closed for further study.

The trail was closed.

The official reason given was maintenance.
