Something lived in the dark forest that was not human and not animal. Those who saw it described eyes like burning coals and a shape that seemed wrong in every way. The disappearances had been happening for years but nobody connected them until now.

Owen was the investigator assigned to look into it. He was told to be careful. He was not told what he was being careful about because nobody knew.

He found out on his second night.

He was camped near the eastern trail when he heard it. Not an animal sound. Something between a growl and a human voice saying words in no language he knew.

His flashlight caught it between the trees. Eight feet tall at least. Four limbs but moving wrong. Its head turned toward the light and he saw the eyes. Six of them. Arranged like flowers.

Owen did not move. The thing did not move. They stayed like that for thirty seconds that felt like thirty years.

Then it stepped back into the darkness and was gone.

Owen filed his report. He wrote that the disappearances were likely due to difficult terrain. He did not write what he had seen. Nobody would have believed him. And he did not want it to know that he had told.
