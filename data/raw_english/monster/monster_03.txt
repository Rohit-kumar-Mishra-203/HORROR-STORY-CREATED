The children started drawing it before the adults knew it existed. Not one child. All of them. In every classroom in the school. On the same day.

A creature with too many legs. A body that did not match any animal in any textbook. Eyes that the children drew as spirals rather than circles as if they could not quite remember the shape correctly.

The school counselor collected the drawings. She was troubled enough to contact a biologist she knew.

The biologist looked at the drawings for a long time. She said the children are describing something consistent. She said the details match across all the drawings in ways that coincidence cannot explain.

She said where did they see this.

The counselor said they said they dreamed it.

All of them. The same night.

The biologist said and you have not seen anything unusual in the area.

The counselor said not yet.

She said that is the word I would focus on.
