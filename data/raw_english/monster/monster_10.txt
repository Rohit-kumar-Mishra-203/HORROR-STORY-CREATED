She had been a forest ranger for twenty years. She had seen bears. Mountain lions. Once an animal she could not identify that disappeared before she could photograph it.

Nothing had prepared her for this.

She found the first signs in spring. Trees damaged at a height of twelve feet. Not lightning damage. Something had done it deliberately. Marking.

She followed the marking pattern deeper into the forest. It led to a valley she had never visited in twenty years.

She stood at the edge of the valley and looked down.

It was down there. In the center of the valley. She could not see it clearly from this distance but she could see it.

It could see her too.

It did not move. It watched her. Patient. As if it had been watching this valley for a very long time and she was simply the latest thing to arrive at the edge of it.

She backed away slowly. She left the valley.

She filed no report. She retired at the end of that year.

She never went back to that forest. But sometimes in the years after she would look at a treeline and feel with absolute certainty that something on the other side of it was looking back.
