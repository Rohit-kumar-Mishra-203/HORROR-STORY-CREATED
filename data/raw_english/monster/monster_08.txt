The expedition had been in the jungle for three weeks when they found the clearing.

Perfect circle. No vegetation. The trees around the edges bent away from it as if they had been growing away from something at the center for a very long time.

In the center a depression in the earth. Old. Very old. As if something very large had rested there.

The expedition leader said make camp away from the clearing. Nobody goes near it after dark.

One member of the team did not follow this instruction. He went to the clearing at midnight with a camera and a recorder.

In the morning he was not in camp.

They found his equipment at the edge of the clearing. Still running. The camera footage showed him walking into the clearing. Standing in the center. Looking up.

The footage showed what he was looking at.

The team packed their equipment and left the jungle without completing their survey.

The footage was submitted to the expedition's sponsors along with a recommendation that the area be designated off limits for further study.

The recommendation was accepted without questions.
