The blood test results came back wrong. Not diseased. Not deficient. Just wrong. Numbers that did not correspond to anything in the medical literature.

Dr. Chen ran the tests three times. The same results each time.

She called her patient back. She said I need to ask you some questions.

He came in the next morning. He sat across from her desk with the same calm expression he had worn at every appointment for the past five years.

She showed him the results. She said these are your blood work numbers. She said can you explain them.

He looked at them for a moment. Then he looked at her.

He said I was wondering when this would come up.

She said how long.

He said a very long time.

She said are you dangerous.

He thought about it seriously. He said I try very hard not to be.

She said that is not the same as no.

He said no. It is not. He said nothing actually living can honestly say no to that question.

She considered this. Then she said I am going to continue being your doctor. She said I am going to need you to be honest with me about your particular medical situation.

He said that seems fair.

She said good. She said your iron levels are still concerning regardless of everything else.
