The bodies had been found drained of blood for three months before anyone suspected the truth. The police had theories. None of them were correct.

Owen was the investigator assigned to the case. He was methodical. He did not believe in anything he could not verify.

By the end of the first week he had a suspect. By the end of the second week he had confirmed the suspect's address. An old house at the edge of the city that had been occupied for as long as city records went back.

He went alone. He should not have gone alone.

The man who answered the door looked about forty. Well dressed. Calm. He invited Owen in without being asked.

Owen sat across from him at a table and asked his questions. The man answered them all. Honestly and in detail.

At the end Owen asked how long he had been in the city.

The man smiled. He said since the beginning. He said your records only go back two hundred years but I was here before the records.

Owen left. He wrote his report. He listed the case as unsolved.

He is still alive. He checks the obituaries every morning. He has been checking them for twenty years. The man's name has not appeared.
