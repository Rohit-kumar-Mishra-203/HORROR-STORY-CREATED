The retirement home had an unusual volunteer. He came every Tuesday and Thursday evening. He talked to the residents. He listened. He remembered everything they told him.

The staff liked him. The residents adored him. He had been coming for eleven years.

One of the nurses did some research. She found records of a volunteer matching his description going back thirty years. Same name. Same physical description. Photograph from thirty years ago showed the same face. No older than forty.

She asked him about it directly one evening.

He looked at her for a long moment. Then he said I like old people. They have interesting stories. They have been places I have not been and seen things I have not seen.

She said but how.

He said everyone makes choices about how to spend their time. This is how I spend some of mine.

She said are you going to hurt anyone.

He said I have not hurt anyone here in eleven years. I do not intend to start.

She believed him. She kept his secret. She still works there. He still comes. She has gotten older. He has not.
