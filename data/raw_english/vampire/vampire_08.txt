Maya spent two years tracking the disappearances before she found the pattern. Not geographic. Not demographic. Something else entirely.

All the missing people had been donors. Blood donors. Registered at the same clinic.

The clinic had been operating for thirty years. Clean records. Good reputation. No complaints.

Maya went undercover as a new donor. Standard procedure. Forms. Blood draw. Brief examination.

The doctor who examined her was unremarkable. Mid fifties. Efficient. Thorough.

He looked at her chart and said everything looks excellent. Then he looked up at her and said you are looking for something.

She said I am a patient.

He said no. He said you have been here before. Not as yourself. As two different people. I recognized the eyes.

She said nothing.

He said I have been running this clinic for thirty years and before that a similar clinic for thirty years before that. I have never taken anything from anyone who did not consent. I have never left anyone harmed.

He said but I know why you are here and you should know that the ones who disappeared were not taken by me.
