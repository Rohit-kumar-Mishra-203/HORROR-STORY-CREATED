The village priest had kept a record. Going back two hundred years. Each priest passing it to the next. A record of one particular family in the village.

They had been there since the record began. The same family. The same house. Always the same number of members. Never more. Never fewer.

The current priest inherited the record and read it carefully. He noted the pattern. Specific family members appearing in records under different names twenty years apart. Thirty years apart. Always the same physical description. Always the same eyes.

He requested a meeting with the family patriarch.

The man who came to the meeting looked about sixty. He sat across from the priest and waited.

The priest put the record on the table. He said I know what you are.

The man looked at the record. He said we have been here a long time.

The priest said I know.

The man said and.

The priest said and we have been here together a long time. My predecessors made their peace with this. I am trying to make mine.

He said what do you need from us.

The man was quiet for a moment. Then he said the same thing you need from us. To be left alone to exist.
