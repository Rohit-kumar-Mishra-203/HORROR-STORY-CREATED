The historian had been studying the family for twenty years. Old European nobility. A lineage that went back further than most. She had found references in documents going back six centuries to the same physical description. Same height. Same features. Same particular shade of grey in the eyes.

She had a theory. She had never said it out loud.

Then she found the portrait. In a private collection. Dated sixteen forty. The man in it was looking directly at the painter with an expression of mild patience.

She recognized him immediately. She had met him at a conference two years ago. He had been presenting a paper on medieval trade routes. He had seemed very knowledgeable.

She requested a meeting.

He agreed. They met in a hotel lobby at nine in the evening. He arrived exactly on time. He was wearing gloves. He ordered nothing.

She put the portrait reproduction on the table between them.

He looked at it for a moment. Then he looked at her.

He said you are the third researcher in fifty years to find me this way.

She said what happened to the other two.

He said they made a choice. The same choice I am about to offer you.
