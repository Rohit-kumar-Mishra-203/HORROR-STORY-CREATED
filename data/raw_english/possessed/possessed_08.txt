It started with small things she could not explain. The oven being on when she had not turned it on. Doors she was certain she had locked found unlocked. Her keys in different places from where she had left them.

She assumed she was tired. Stressed. Forgetting things.

Then her friend came to visit and said you seem different.

She asked in what way.

Her friend said I cannot put my finger on it. She said the way you're sitting. Your posture. It's not how you usually sit.

She had not noticed her posture.

She started paying attention to herself. She began keeping notes.

The notes showed patterns she did not recognize. Preferences she had not had before. A sudden interest in music she had always disliked. Food she had never eaten suddenly tasting right. Routes she had never taken somehow feeling familiar.

She wrote in her notes something is learning how to be me through me.

The next morning the words had been added to. In her own handwriting.

The addition said I am trying my best. I apologize for the inconvenience.
