The village elder told them what to watch for. The signs that something had come in with someone who had visited the old site.

They would be tired for no reason. They would lose time. Small amounts at first. An hour here. An afternoon there.

They would begin to know things they had not been told. Small things. The name of a stranger before being introduced. The content of a letter before it was opened.

They would wake at the same time every night. The same hour. Without an alarm.

The elder said if you see these signs in someone you know bring them to me.

Two weeks later a young man showed all four signs.

The elder sat with him for a day and a night.

In the morning the elder said he is clear.

The young man said thank you.

The elder said do not thank me. She said whatever was in you was not trying to harm you. She said it was trying to learn from you. She said it had been without a body for a very long time and it missed the experience of simply being alive.

The young man said where did it go.

The elder said I do not know. She said probably to wait. She said the old ones are patient. They have learned to be.
