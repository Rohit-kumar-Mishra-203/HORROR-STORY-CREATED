The exorcism was the third attempt. The first two had failed. The priest who conducted the third one said afterward that he had never encountered a case quite like it.

The thing inside the boy was cooperative. That was the word he used. Cooperative.

It answered questions clearly. It explained what it was. Where it had come from. How it had gotten in.

It said the boy had been dreaming of it for a year before it arrived. It said dreams were a kind of invitation that most people did not realize they were extending.

It said it meant no harm. It was simply looking for somewhere to exist.

The priest asked it to leave.

It said where would I go.

The priest said I cannot help you with that.

It was quiet for a moment. Then it said that is fair. He said that is honest at least.

It left. The boy woke up. He remembered everything.

The priest asked him about the dreams.

The boy said I know it sounds strange but I feel bad for it. He said it just wanted to be somewhere.
