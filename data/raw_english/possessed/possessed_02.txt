The possession was not violent. That was what made it so difficult to recognize.

He still went to work. Still made jokes. Still remembered their anniversary and her coffee order and the name of her sister's dog.

But there were gaps. Small ones. Moments where he paused in the middle of a sentence and seemed to be listening to something she could not hear.

There were the dreams he described. Always the same location. A dark place he could not quite describe. And a conversation he was having with something he could not quite describe.

He said it was curious about everything. He said it asked him questions about being alive. What food tasted like. What it felt like to be warm. What sleep was like from the inside.

She asked him what he told it.

He paused in that new way of his and said I told it everything it wanted to know.

She asked him how long it had been there.

He thought about it for a long moment.

He said I am not entirely sure I am the right person to ask.
