The child was seven when it started. His parents noticed he was spending long periods doing nothing. Just sitting and looking at a point in the middle distance.

When they asked him what he was looking at he said I am listening.

They asked listening to what.

He said to the person who talks to me.

They were worried but the doctors found nothing. They said it was imagination. They said some children had elaborate internal lives.

Three months later he began repeating things that no seven year old should know. Historical facts. Names of people long dead. Detailed descriptions of places he had never been.

His parents started recording these sessions. They had the recordings analyzed.

A historian said the details the boy provided about a specific location and time period were accurate. Too accurate for coincidence. Too specific for the information to have been available to a child through normal means.

He said I do not know what your son is. But whatever is speaking through him was there. It was present at these events. It is describing first hand experience.

The parents asked what they should do.

The historian said ask it what it wants.

They asked.

The boy was quiet for a moment. Then he said it wants someone to know it was here. It wants to be remembered. It has been forgotten for a very long time and it does not like being forgotten.
