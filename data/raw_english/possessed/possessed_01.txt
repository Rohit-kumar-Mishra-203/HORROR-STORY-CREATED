Nobody noticed the change in Elena until the third day.

She had gone to the old house on a dare. She came back an hour later and said she was fine. She looked fine. She sounded fine. But something was different.

Her sister noticed it first. The way Elena paused before answering simple questions. As if she had to think about what a normal person would say.

Her mother noticed the eyes. Not the color. The expression. Like someone looking out through a window from inside a room.

On the fifth night her sister woke to find Elena standing in the doorway of her bedroom. At three in the morning. Not moving. Just standing there in the dark.

Her sister asked what are you doing.

Elena said in a voice that was almost right I am just watching you sleep.

The priest came the next morning. He said there is something in this house that was not here before. He said it came in with your daughter but it is not your daughter.

He was right about the first part. He was wrong about one thing. It had not come in with Elena. Elena had not come home at all.
