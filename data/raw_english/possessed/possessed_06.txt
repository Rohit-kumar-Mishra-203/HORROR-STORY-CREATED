She woke up in the hospital. She had been there for three days. She had no memory of the three days.

Her husband explained. She had been found at the edge of the city. Walking. Barefoot. In the direction of the old church that had been deconsecrated and abandoned thirty years ago.

She did not remember walking there. She did not remember the three days. She remembered going to bed on a Tuesday and then the hospital on Friday.

But she felt different. Not ill. Not confused. Different in a way she could not articulate. Like being in a room you know well and noticing that all the furniture has been moved by two inches. Everything is technically in the right place but nothing is quite where you expected it to be.

The hospital ran tests. Nothing abnormal.

A priest came to see her. He sat with her for two hours. He asked her questions she had trouble answering. What year was she born. What was her mother's name. Simple things that she knew but had to reach for.

He said I think something came back from that church with you.

She said what do you mean.

He said I mean that you went there and something came back in you. He said I have seen this before. He said the important question is whether you want it there.

She thought about it for longer than she expected to.

She said honestly I am not sure yet.
