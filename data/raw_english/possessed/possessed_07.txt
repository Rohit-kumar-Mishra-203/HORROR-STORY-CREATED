The medium had been doing readings for twenty years. She had a policy. Never let anything in. She could feel presences. She could relay messages. But she never opened herself as a channel.

She broke the rule once.

A grieving mother. A dead child. Fourteen years old. An accident six months ago. The mother desperate. The medium moved.

She opened.

The child came through. She relayed the message. The mother wept. It was real. It was contact.

The child did not leave.

She thought she had closed the channel. She was wrong about that.

For a month she felt it. A young presence. Following her. Not malevolent. Just there. The way a child follows a parent through a house. Not asking for anything. Just not wanting to be alone.

She talked to it. She said you have to go. She said your mother is going to be alright.

The presence faded after another week.

She kept the rule after that. Even when she was moved. Even when she was asked. She said I have learned that some doors once opened are very slow to close.
